# Quick Reference: Document Request System

## For Students 👨‍🎓

### The 3 Documents You Can Request:

| Document | Use Case | Processing Time |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| 📋 **Transcript of Records** | Job applications, scholarships, further studies | 6 working days |
| 📄 **Transfer Credentials & Academic Records** | Transferring to another institution, employment verification | 6 working days |
| 🎓 **Second Copy of Diploma** | Replacement for lost/damaged original | 6 working days |

### Step-by-Step Process:

1. **Go to Dashboard** → Click "Request Document"
2. **Select Document Type** from the dropdown
3. **Provide Purpose** - Why do you need this document?
4. **Choose Quantity** - How many copies?
5. **Submit** - Your request is now in the system
6. **View Timeline** - See expected completion date on your request details
7. **Wait for Notification** - Email when document is ready (around day 6)
8. **Schedule Appointment** - Choose a convenient pickup time
9. **Attend Appointment** - Pick up your document

### Important Dates:
- **Request Date**: When you submit your request
- **Expected Completion**: Request date + 6 working days
- **Appointment Date**: Scheduled by registrar after document is ready

### Tips:
- ✅ Submit requests early if you have deadlines
- ✅ Check your email regularly for updates
- ✅ Arrive 5-10 minutes early to your appointment
- ✅ Keep your request confirmation number

## For Registrars 👨‍💼

### Processing Workflow:

```
PENDING → PROCESSING → READY → [APPOINTMENT SCHEDULED] → COMPLETED
  ↓
[Process document 
 over 6 days]
```

### Timeline Management:

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Day 1 | Receive request, begin processing |
| Days 2-5 | Continue document preparation |
| Day 6 | Complete processing, mark "Ready" |
| Day 6+ | Schedule appointment with student |
| Appointment Day | Complete transaction |

### Creating an Appointment:

1. **Registrar Portal** → "Create Appointment"
2. **Remember**: Only schedule after processing period is near/complete
3. **Select Student** and their **Document Request**
4. **Choose Date & Time** - Coordinate with student availability
5. **Add Remarks** - Any special notes
6. **Save** - Notification sent to student automatically

### Status Updates:

- **Pending**: Just received, awaiting processing
- **Processing**: Being prepared (don't schedule appointment yet!)
- **Ready**: Completed and waiting for pickup appointment
- **Completed**: Picked up by student
- **Rejected**: Not approved (requires rejection reason)

### Document Checklist:
- [ ] Document request received and logged
- [ ] Started processing on Day 1
- [ ] Processing completed by Day 6
- [ ] Marked "Ready" in system
- [ ] Appointment scheduled with student
- [ ] Student attended appointment
- [ ] Document delivered
- [ ] Marked "Completed"

## Common Questions ❓

### Q: Why 6 working days?
**A**: This allows sufficient time to prepare, verify, and quality-check documents properly.

### Q: What are "working days"?
**A**: Monday through Friday. Weekends are not counted.

### Q: Can I get it faster?
**A**: Minimum processing time is 6 working days for all documents.

### Q: Do I need an appointment?
**A**: Yes, all documents require an appointment for pickup.

### Q: Can I reschedule my appointment?
**A**: Yes, contact the registrar's office to reschedule.

### Q: When will I be notified?
**A**: You'll receive an email when your document is ready (around working day 6).

### Q: What if my request is rejected?
**A**: You'll receive an email with the rejection reason and can submit a new request.

### Q: Can I request multiple copies?
**A**: Yes, up to 10 copies in a single request.
